# Grilling Discipline

Maintain a live decision tree.

For each branch: state the decision, inspect evidence, explain why it matters, recommend a default, ask one question, record the answer, and follow the exposed branch.

Prioritize questions that can invalidate the solution or change scope, safety, value, or phase order.

Stop only when load-bearing branches are resolved, remaining unknowns are non-blocking, and facts, assumptions, and decisions are separated.

Never fill an operational-fact gap with confidence.
